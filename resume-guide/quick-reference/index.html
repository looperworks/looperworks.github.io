Quick Reference Card

Header
Name (largest text), professional email, phone, city/state, portfolio URL. No objective statement. No full mailing address.
Education
Degree, institution, date. GPA if 3.0+. Relevant coursework (4-6). Thesis title if applicable. NAAB/LAAB accreditation noted.
Experience
Reverse chronological. Action verb + task + context + outcome. 2-4 bullets per position. Quantify everything you can.
Projects
2-4 strongest studio projects. Include scale, program, site, and your role. Flag competition entries or real-client work.
Skills
Organized by category. Only tools you can use professionally. Most relevant tools first. Include GIS for landscape architecture roles.
Activities
AIAS, ASLA, NOMAS, competitions, community design-build, teaching. Keep brief, 4 to 8 lines maximum.
THE GOLDEN RULE: Every line on your resume should answer one question. Does this prove I can contribute to this firm from day one? If it does not, cut it or rewrite it.
Prepared for architecture and landscape architecture students. Based on faculty critique notes, AIA and ASLA career resources, and real student resume examples.
